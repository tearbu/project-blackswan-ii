## Part 2: Conflicting-figures register

Each entry gives the competing values and their sources, plus the **presentation rule** this assessment applies. The rule is binding on all artefacts in this project (report, registers, board briefing, deck).

### CF-01: Total stolen
- **Values:** ~$6.34B (Binance, E-01); $6.36B (TRM, E-04); ~$6.4B (Chainalysis, E-03); $6.43B (Elliptic, E-07); "more than $6 billion" (FBI E-02; DOJ E-05). Superseded figures still circulating: ~$5.9B (an early secondary summary of Chainalysis) and ~$5.57B (first-night on-chain tallies) (E-23).
- **Why they differ:** valuation moment (theft-time vs later prices, TRM itself gives $6.23B at later prices) and chain count (CF-05).
- **Presentation rule:** state the range **$6.34B to $6.43B with attribution**. Never use a single unqualified figure. Never use the superseded $5.9B/$5.57B values except when documenting the figure history.

### CF-02: Total frozen / blocked
- **Values:** $2.54B by 23:35 UTC 29 Oct and ~$3B in 30 Oct updates (Binance, via E-10); ~$2.7B (TRM, E-04; DOJ "more than $2.7 billion", E-05); ~$2.8B (Chainalysis, E-03); $2.85B (Binance E-01; Elliptic E-07). Of the frozen total, >$1.2B is under court-authorised seizure/restraint (E-05).
- **Why they differ:** cut-off time and definition ("frozen" vs "frozen or blocked/intercepted").
- **Presentation rule:** state **~$2.7B to $2.85B**, note the definitional spread, and always keep "frozen", "court-restrained" and "recovered" as separate measures. Frozen is never added to coverage or described as recovered.

### CF-03: Coverage progression (material restatement)
- **Contemporaneous sequence (as announced):** ~80% (30 Oct) → 90% (31 Oct) → 95% (1 Nov) → ~98% (4 Nov) → "coverage complete" (12 Nov) (E-08, E-09, E-10).
- **Conflicting restatement:** the 3 November preliminary report states **98% replenished as of 31 October** and "more than 100% of the funds [retained]". On its face, that cannot be reconciled with the contemporaneous 90% (31 Oct) and 95% (1 Nov) announcements (E-01). Possible explanations (UTC+8 dating, later restatement) are not given by the operator.
- **Presentation rule:** use the **contemporaneous sequence** as the record. Cite the 3 Nov restatement only as a flagged discrepancy and as evidence for risk R-15 (disclosure consistency). "Coverage" is always described as **reserve replenishment from SAFU + Futures Insurance Funds + corporate treasury** (company money replacing stolen assets), never as recovery.

### CF-04: First public acknowledgement time
- **Values:** ~21:15 UTC 29 Oct for first CZ/Binance posts (LunarCrush reconstruction, https://lunarcrush.com/research/binance-hack-reconstructed/LunarCrush-Binance-Hack-Reconstructed.pdf); 00:24 UTC 30 Oct for the first executive use of "exploit" and 02:23 to 02:25 UTC for the formal update (CryptoTimes, E-26). All sources agree acknowledgement came the same night.
- **Presentation rule:** state that acknowledgement came **the same night, with the exact first-acknowledgement time unsettled across sources**. Do not publish a single time. This conflict is distinct from the operator's *detection* time, which is simply undisclosed (NV-01).

### CF-05: Number of blockchains affected
- **Values:** 10 (TRM, E-04; Chainalysis, E-03); 11 (Elliptic, E-07). Likely a counting/timing difference (a second wave ~23:05 UTC reached Avalanche/Polygon wallets).
- **Presentation rule:** "10 to 11 blockchains, by source."

### CF-06: Net outflow figures
- **Values:** net −$3.64B in the first 9.5 hours of reopening (Binance on X via Reuters, E-16 context); net $4.72B withdrawn as of 2 Nov (Binance to Reuters/BBC, E-16); $10.97B gross withdrawals 1 Nov to early 3 Nov with net −$5.28B over 46 hours (Teng figures, E-17 context). Separately, ~28,316 BTC net wallet outflows to 5 Nov (Santiment, E-18), a different unit and measure, carrying Santiment's pre-hack-decline caveat.
- **Presentation rule:** never sum or rank these. Each is quoted **with its window and source**, and the $4.72B / $5.28B pair is flagged as non-reconcilable as published.

### CF-07: Ripple/RLUSD assistance figure
- **Values:** Yi He credited Ripple with helping stop **~$200M** moving; TRM could confirm only **40.5M RLUSD frozen on-chain** (of 88.5M RLUSD taken) (E-04).
- **Presentation rule:** use TRM's confirmed on-chain figure. The ~$200M executive claim is recorded as unverified.

### CF-08: Proof-of-reserves proof statuses (time-sensitive)
- **31 Oct snapshot (10 tokens):** proof **completed 2 Nov** (Binance Recovery Update #5, via E-10).
- **4 Nov snapshot (44 tokens):** proof estimated at 50 to 60 hours (E-08). **No completion announcement located** as of research. Status: *unknown*, not "failed" and not "complete".
- **12 Nov snapshot (198 tokens):** proof estimated at 40 to 50 hours from 12 Nov 00:00 UTC, i.e. due **13 Nov 16:00 to 14 Nov 02:00 UTC**. **Pending, and not yet due**, at the research moment (E-09, E-32).
- **Presentation rule:** each snapshot's proof status is stated **separately and with its date**. No blanket statement that "the proof of reserves is verified" is permitted in any artefact.

### CF-09: Incident dating and timezones
- Binance's preliminary report is dated in **UTC+8**, where the incident falls on 30 October (01:15 to 07:56). In UTC the theft ran **29 Oct 17:15 to 23:56**, with the main burst from 20:05 UTC (E-01, E-04). Secondary summaries quoting "17:15 to 19:56" without a timezone are quoting the UTC+8 infiltration window converted.
- **Presentation rule:** all times in this assessment are **UTC**, converted where necessary, and Binance's "October 30" headline dating is noted wherever the report is cited.

## Part 3: Could not verify / still unknown

The following could not be established from any credible source as of 13 November 2026. Each one is carried in the report as an open item, not as a finding.

- **NV-01: Operator detection time.** When Binance's own monitoring first alerted, and whether internal detection preceded external on-chain reporting (~8 minutes after the 20:20 UTC delegation, per LunarCrush). The preliminary report states only that an "internal alert about large transfers" was received, with no time (E-01).
- **NV-02: Amount actually recovered.** No Binance, DOJ or analytics source discloses any amount of stolen assets recovered and returned. Frozen (CF-02), court-restrained (>$1.2B, E-05) and coverage (CF-03) are not recovery.
- **NV-03: Internal AI tool identity and abuse method.** The tool is unnamed. No prompt-injection, MCP or other mechanism has been identified publicly. How it conferred action over two further engineers' machines is unexplained (E-01, E-13).
- **NV-04: Segmentation and signing-approval design.** Why a developer endpoint could reach peer machines via internal tooling. Why a standing deployment token was stored on a laptop. What network segmentation existed. And whether signing requests were validated against approved withdrawals (E-13, E-29 record the questions; no source answers them).
- **NV-05: 4 November ZK proof completion.** No completion announcement located (CF-08).
- **NV-06: 12 November ZK proof verification.** Pending and not yet due at research time. Due window 13 Nov 16:00 to 14 Nov 02:00 UTC (CF-08).
- **NV-07: Full withdrawal reopening date.** Withdrawals were still phased on 12 November. An independent check on 11 November counted ~620 pairs on 55 networks open (~43%). No full-reopening date has been announced (E-09, E-10).
- **NV-08: Final coverage allocation and SAFU replenishment.** The split of coverage funding among SAFU / Futures Insurance Funds / treasury has not been disclosed. SAFU's drawdown (~7,499 BTC out on 31 Oct, leaving ~7,500 BTC, per Cointelegraph reporting of 2 Nov) is consistent with a partial deployment of roughly half the fund. Completion of the promised replenishment to ~$1B is unconfirmed (E-09).
- **NV-09: Final post-mortem and independent review.** The detailed post-mortem promised 30 October and the invited independent review have not been published. No date has been announced (E-01 context, E-10).
- **NV-10: Non-US regulatory response.** No statement or action by any non-US regulator concerning this incident was located. This is recorded as a research gap, not as proof none exists.
- **NV-11: Private verification of the endpoint narrative.** The June/September/October intrusion path rests on the operator's account. Independent analyses are re-readings of it plus on-chain data (E-13).
- **NV-12: Personnel matters.** The departure of CFO Kaiser Ng, announced 7 November, has no established connection to this incident in any source. It is excluded from the assessment's findings and recorded here only to prevent its importation from secondary commentary.
